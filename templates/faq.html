FAQs

Useful answers before we talk.

A few common questions about how Toror works.

Do I need an account to use the website?

No. The public website is open. There is no visitor registration or visitor login.

Can Toror build a system specifically for our organisation?

Yes. The services are designed around practical requirements, existing workflows, and the people who will use the system.

Can you work on a system that already exists?

Yes. Existing software can be reviewed for interface improvements, functionality, deployment issues, maintenance, or extensions.

Do you support deployment after development?

Deployment and ongoing maintenance can be part of the engagement, depending on the project.

How do I request a quotation?

Send an enquiry with the business context, the problem to solve, and any important requirements. That gives the team enough information to respond appropriately.

How are Toror certificates verified?

Certificates issued from the private administration workspace carry a unique certificate number and a verification QR code. The public verification page checks the recorded certificate and its tamper-evident signature.
